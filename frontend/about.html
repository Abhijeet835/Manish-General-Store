Our Roots

A Single Shelf of Dreams,
In the Heart of Bundu.

It began with a modest shopfront on the lanes of Purana Bazar Toli. In 1998, Nandulal Gorai opened a small general store with a clear promise: that every product on his shelves would be something he'd be proud to put on his own family's table.

The neighbourhood quickly noticed. Regulars came not just for the groceries, but for the warmth — a store where your name was remembered, your preferences known, and a short wait was always worth it.

"We didn't just sell groceries. We became part of daily life — the morning atta, the evening milk, the festival sweets. That's what we've always been."
🌱

Two Decades Later

Growing Alongside
Every Household.

As Bundu grew, so did we. The shelves expanded, the range deepened, and generations of the same families continued walking through our doors. From new parents stocking up on essentials to students grabbing a quick snack — we were there for every life moment.

We kept one thing constant through it all: the personal relationship between us and our community. No faceless corporation. Just your neighbourhood store, run with care by people who live here too.

Founder & Co-founder
The Family Behind the Store

Founded by Nandulal Gorai,
Co-led by Manish Gorai.

Nandulal Gorai started this store in 1998 with nothing more than a deep understanding of what his neighbours needed and an unshakeable belief that running an honest business was its own reward. He grew up in Bundu, and this store has always been personal to him — not just a livelihood, but a way of giving back.

Twenty-six years on, that philosophy still guides every decision. Today, his son Manish Gorai (co-founder) has joined the helm, bringing the family business into the digital age in 2026. The store has grown, but its values have not changed by a single rupee's worth.
